Room file guidance: rm_mountain_downhill

Create a new room named rm_mountain_downhill with resolution 1024x4096 (tall vertical track).
Layers and placements:
- obj_ground covering the room
- Place narrow path of ramps along the vertical axis at various y positions to simulate downhill
- Start obj_bike at top: x=400, y=128
- Place obj_camera and UI objects
- Add obj_ramp and obj_rock obstacles along the path

Tip: use small room sections and connect them with ramps to simulate downhill flow.
